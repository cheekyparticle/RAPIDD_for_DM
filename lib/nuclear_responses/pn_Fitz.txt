# Fit coefficients of the nuclear responses of arXiv:1203.3542 (Fitzpatrick et al.). Outdated, as they
#   diverge at high recoil energies (E_R > 100 keV): iso_Fitz has the fits of DMFormFactor v6.
# The rows of 19F, 23Na, 70-76Ge, 127I and 128-136Xe are those of App. A.3 of arXiv:1203.3542 (LaTeX source of
#   v3), checked line by line on 2026-11-24, when 77 rows were corrected: the 19F Sigma'' rows (copies of the
#   Sigma' rows), the a0 of the M rows (Z^2, Z N, N^2 instead of the rounded values of the paper, e.g. 1024
#   instead of 1000 for 70Ge p p), signs and digits of single coefficients (e.g. the y^7 coefficient of the
#   Phi'' rows of I and Xe, a3 of 127I M n n) and shifted series (131Xe Sigma'' p p, 129Xe Delta n n).
#   The paper gives 2-3 significant digits: F_M(0) differs from Z^2, Z N, N^2 by up to 2-3%.

#
# F_{O1,O2}^{N1,N2}(E_R) = prefactor * exp(-2y) * sum_{k=0}^{10} a_k y^k,  y = (q b / 2)^2,  q^2 = 2 m_T E_R,
#   with b^2 the harmonic-oscillator length squared of the nucleus (B2 in lib/source/effFormFact.c) and
#   m_T the mass of the nucleus (A, Z). Proton-neutron basis: N1, N2 = p, n.
# Responses (O1, O2): M = M, Sp = Sigma', Spp = Sigma'', D = Delta, Ppp = Phi'', Pp = Phi', Pt (Pp and Pt
